EZSVS Your First Day v0.4.3-web.1

Open https://zsyuyiie.github.io/ezsvs-onboarding/ and choose English. Use WASD/click to move, right-drag to look, E to interact, G for the objective and H for the handbook. Touch users should use landscape orientation, the left movement stick, and right-side look area.

Progress is local to this browser; export it before clearing data or moving devices. The same URL and save schema are retained. See README.md for validation, full Unity source download and device-testing limits.
